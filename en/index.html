YOUR DAILY PRAYER COMPANION

Shia prayer times for everyday life.

NOOR is a free Shia Muslim prayer app for Android and iPhone, with Ja'fari-inspired prayer times for Swedish cities or your location, Qibla direction and tools for building a regular prayer routine.

Features for your daily prayers

Prayer times and Qibla

Find Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha, sunrise and the direction to Mecca. Calculated times are estimates; check your local Shia community's calendar.

Ja'fari prayer and wudu guides

Illustrated prayer instructions, Arabic phrases and pronunciation support, a dua library and Tasbih Fatima al-Zahra. The guides are educational aids, not a substitute for religious guidance.

Prayer journal and personal garden

Record prayers, track streaks and unlock decorations. Keep a Qada count, view the Islamic calendar and track fasting days.

Prayer reminders

The web app can play Adhan while it is open and offers calendar export for system alerts. It cannot reliably play Adhan when the screen is locked. Native Android features differ from those of the web app.

Install NOOR for free

On iPhone, open the site in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use the web app or download the separate APK from the installation section.
